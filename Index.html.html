How to use

1. Connect headphones. 2. Tap Start microphone and allow access. 3. Select a preset and adjust the controls. Your processed voice plays through your headphones.

This free prototype changes voice tone with EQ and a subtle effect; it does not perform full AI gender conversion or feed audio into Instagram/SIM calls. iPhone apps/websites cannot replace the system microphone for those calls.
